Questions

What brokers ask first.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to Meta from your own account. We never touch your ad money. Our fee is separate and you only pay it once you get results.

What if you do not deliver?

You pay us nothing. We keep working, or you can stop at any time. The ad money already spent on Meta stays with Meta, so we agree a monthly ad budget in writing before we start.

Is a bot talking to my buyers?

No. Reminders and confirmations are automatic, but the conversation that decides whether a lead is real is with a person on our team. Buyers have told us they do not want an AI qualifying them.

Are the leads exclusive?

Yes. One broker per project, and a lead is never resold. Each passport says who it was issued to.

Do I need a Trakheesi permit?

Yes. Every Dubai property ad needs one, and off-plan ads also need the developer's NOC. We put the number on every ad and check the project's public record before any ad runs.

Do you promise sales?

No. We deliver verified buyer leads. What your agents do with them, and the market, decide the sales. We show you how fast each lead was called so nothing is hidden.

Can solo agents work with you?

Not yet. We work with RERA-registered brokerages that have an ad budget and at least a few agents.
