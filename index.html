Some enquiries never get an answer.

Researchers sent a test enquiry to 2,241 US companies. Almost a quarter never replied, and only 37% replied within an hour. The same article found that firms that contacted a lead within the hour were nearly seven times more likely to have a real conversation with a decision-maker than firms that waited one more hour. That was 2011, and it was the US. We wouldn't bet that buyers in Dubai are more patient.

How it works

We read the market before we spend your money.

We call it ATLAS, because five letters are easier to remember than five bullet points. The idea is simple: research first, ads second, and a check on every lead before it counts.

AnalyseStep 1

We pull more than a thousand live ads from Meta's Ad Library and look hardest at the ones that have run the longest, because an ad that survives is usually making money. Then we write up your project: who buys it, what they ask, what puts them off.

A
TailorStep 2

Ads with real people and real numbers on screen: price, payment plan, handover date. Not the developer's brochure.

LayerStep 3

We start small and test a few ads. Then we put more of your budget behind whichever one brings in leads that pass our checks. Not the cheapest form fill. The cheapest verified lead.

AskStep 4

The form asks for budget and timeline, and we use Meta's "higher intent" form type, which Meta says is focused on "very intentional leads". A short page about your project sits behind it.

Questions

What brokers usually ask.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to Meta, from your own account. We never touch your ad money. What you pay us is separate, and it's covered by the money-back guarantee.

What if you don't get me 15?

You get back what you paid us, or we keep working until we reach 15. Your choice. The ad money already spent on Meta stays with Meta, because we never held it. That's why we agree a monthly ad budget in writing before we start.

Are the leads exclusive?

Yes. One broker per project, and we never resell a lead. Each Lead Passport says who it was issued to.

Do I need a Trakheesi permit?

Yes. Dubai property ads need a permit number, and off-plan ads also need the developer's NOC. We put the number on every ad and check the project's public record before anything runs. The fines for getting it wrong are real.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. We can guarantee 15 qualified leads, each one checked, or your money back. What happens next depends on the market and on how well your agents follow up. You'll see how fast every lead was called, so nothing is hidden.

Can solo agents work with you?

Not yet. We work with RERA-registered brokerages that have an ad budget and at least a few agents.
